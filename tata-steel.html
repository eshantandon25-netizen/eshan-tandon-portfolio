Ownership & assistance

What I personally did.

My role: Individual student analyst.

My contribution: Collected financial figures, reviewed AI-assisted interpretations, selected commentary, and submitted the workbook.

AI assistance: Ratio calculations, Excel formula creation, and interpretation of results. I had not independently checked the formulas before preparing this portfolio.

Verification during portfolio preparation

A separate arithmetic review independently evaluated 183 internal formulas and matched their saved results. This does not establish that the source figures or every ratio definition are correct.

Another 52 formulas depend on an external workbook and were not independently recalculated. These checks were completed during portfolio preparation and are not represented as my original assignment work.

Source discrepancy: I recall using Screener, while the workbook attributes several tables to StockAnalysis / S&P Global. Resolving this attribution is To Be Added.

Evidence quality

What needs checking next.

Financial figures and conclusions

Reconcile the five-year figures to original financial statements or a saved source export. The entered revenue figures finish below the starting year, so the earlier description of consistently growing revenue is not used as a verified conclusion.

Ratio interpretation and scope

Capacity utilisation commentary says “near-full” while the workbook calculates roughly 56–66%. A metric combines Group EBITDA with India-only production; scope must be aligned before using it as a performance measure. Profit-growth percentages across loss-to-profit years also need clearer interpretation.

Supporting artifacts and source access

Assignment brief, original source export, exact annual-report references, and any faculty feedback are To Be Added. The submitted Cash Flow sheet references another workbook, which must be resolved before the deliverable is described as self-contained.
